# Rule of Life

A personal daily rule for iPhone: prayer hours, the Orthodox fasting calendar, habits, daily lessons, a conduct path, gym and calisthenics training, and a zero-based budget with subscriptions and savings advice.

It runs as a home-screen app. It works offline, and all entries are stored on the phone.

## Put it online (free, one time)

1. Sign in at github.com (create a free account if needed).
2. Tap **+** then **New repository**. Name it `rule-of-life`, choose **Public**, and tap **Create repository**.
3. Tap **uploading an existing file**. Select all seven files from this folder: `index.html`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `README.md`. Tap **Commit changes**.
4. Open **Settings**, then **Pages**. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
5. After a minute or two the page shows your address: `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/rule-of-life/`

Only the app is public. Your entries never leave your phone.

## Install it on your iPhone

1. Open your address in **Safari** (it must be Safari).
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Rule of Life from the home-screen icon. It opens full-screen like any app.

## Keep your entries safe

- Open **Review**, scroll to **Your data**, and tap **Back up now**. Choose **Save to Files** and pick iCloud Drive.
- The app reminds you on the Today screen if a week passes without a backup.
- To move to a new phone, install the app there, then tap **Restore from a backup** and choose your latest file.
- Deleting the home-screen app also deletes its entries, so back up first.

## Updating the app

Upload a new `index.html` to the same repository. The app picks up the new version the next time it opens with an internet connection. Your entries are not affected.
